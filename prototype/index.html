Staying at the Top of AFL

From Season Results to Lasting Success

I compared all 18 AFL clubs across 14 seasons (2012–2025) to explore which teams stayed near the top and how that consistency translated into premierships.

🏉Who reached finals? 🗺️Where did clubs succeed? 🏆Who won the most titles? 📈How did results change?

Finals appearances vary across clubs.

A season can be a breakthrough, a rebuild or a missed opportunity. The ladder puts every club’s journey on the same scale.

The ladder ranks clubs after the regular season. First is best. Finals are the end-of-season series that decides the premier.

Success varies across states and cities.

Victoria has ten clubs. Most other states in the competition have two. Raw totals tell only part of the story.

Compare the share of clubs reaching finals in each season to account for the different numbers of clubs.

68%

of NSW club-seasons ended with a finals appearance across 2012–2025.

Sydney and GWS combined for 19 finals appearances across 28 club-seasons. Victoria’s ten clubs reached finals in 43% of their combined club-seasons.

This describes the clubs in each state. It does not measure grassroots participation or the state’s population.

More finals appearances do not guarantee titles.

Getting to finals keeps the door open. Winning the premiership is a different test.

Hawthorn and Richmond each won three titles. Geelong reached finals more often than either, but won once.

A dynasty is a peak.
Consistency is
the whole landscape.

Hawthorn won three in a row, 2013–2015. Richmond won in 2017, 2019 and 2020. Brisbane’s 2024 and 2025 titles are the latest consecutive wins in this study.

Club win rates rise and fall over time.

One average can hide a changing club. Compare the spread of season results, then split the fourteen years into two equal periods.

A steady record can mean sustained success—or sustained struggle. Read consistency alongside performance.

Different clubs lead on different measures of success.

Geelong leads for finals appearances. Hawthorn and Richmond lead for titles. Brisbane shows how much a club can change.

There is more than one way to build a successful era.
